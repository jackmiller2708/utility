## Direction contract

THESIS: Utility Web is a risograph print studio, not a developer dashboard — every transformation is a job run through the press (one spot-color pass per format/size), refusing the dark-panel-with-neon-accent look every AI media tool ships by default.

OWN-WORLD: Two-surface system — a warm near-black "press room" canvas (`#16130F`) carries only chrome and navigation, while every working surface (forms, preview, telemetry) sits on warm cream "paper" (`#F6F0E3`/`#FCF8ED`) with near-black ink text (`#221D16`). Spot color is a small named ink set — Riso Pink `#FF3EA5` (primary action), Riso Blue `#0078BF` (info/secondary), Mint/Gold/Red (success/warning/danger) — one dominant ink per view, always overprinted with dark or light text per the Overprint Rule. Archivo (900 display / 400 body) plus Courier Prime for all trusted numbers (sizes, dimensions, hashes). Square-cut corners (1–4px radius), deckle-edge borders, registration-cross marks standing in for loading/focus/alignment states, hard "paper lift" offset shadows instead of glows.

STORY: A designer opens the tool, drags a file onto a blank "plate," sees it and its live parameters as paper sheets on a press-room floor, tunes format/fit/size on stamped preset tiles and a ruled input sheet, watches a registration cross align while the job runs, and pulls the finished comparison off the press as two ledger-tagged sheets (raw vs. output) before downloading.

FIRST VIEWPORT: The image-resize workspace — press-room floor with a 260px plate rail (tool tabs) on the left; the job sheet fills the remaining width as one paper card split 5/7 (grid grid-cols-1 lg:grid-cols-12 gap-8): parameter form (fit mode, dimensions, format, presets as stamped tiles) at 5 columns, live preview plus raw/output ledger comparison at 7 columns, both floating on the dark canvas with a hard 2px/3px offset shadow and a small pink registration cross in the active panel's corner.

FORM: Riso Zine Studio — assigned direction, index 7 of the grounded list (the roll's assignment), confirmed by the user over two challenger alternates (Creator-Hardware Keycap Bench, Camcorder Viewfinder HUD) and the reviewer's own top pick (Contact Sheet & Loupe).
